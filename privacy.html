Evenphase

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Evenphase is an iPhone app: a private daily diary for premenstrual mood and body symptoms. It is made by CORELABS S.L. ("we"). This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

  • Everything you enter in Evenphase stays on your iPhone. We never receive it.
  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • There are no ads and no trackers in the app.
  • The only information that leaves your phone is what is needed to sell and manage the subscription.

What stays on your iPhone

Your first name (if you give one), your answers to the opening questions, your daily ratings, bleeding and period dates, cycle markers, the treatments you log, your notes, your answers to the optional safety question, your plan for hard days (including any names and phone numbers you type into it) and your reports are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. We have no server for this information and no way to see it.

The app checks some of what you enter in order to show you support (for example, if you rate feeling hopeless very high, or write certain words in a note). That check happens only on your iPhone. Nothing is sent anywhere, and nobody is notified.

Like every app's data, what you enter is included in your own iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer) if you have backups turned on. Those backups are yours and are handled by Apple under your Apple account.

Reminders are created on your iPhone itself and always use neutral words on your lock screen. A report, an export or a copy of your plan is a file you make yourself and send wherever you choose; we never receive a copy. The app does not read your contacts, your location or your Apple Health data.

What leaves your iPhone, and why

To sell and manage the subscription, the app uses two services. Neither receives anything you entered.

  • Apple. Apple takes your payment and manages your subscription under your Apple ID. We never see your card or your Apple ID.
  • Superwall (Superwall, Inc., United States) shows the subscription screen and tells the app whether your subscription is active. It receives a random identifier created for the app on your phone, your purchase history for Evenphase, events about the subscription screen (for example that it was shown, or that a purchase was started), and basic technical details needed to provide the service (such as your device model, iOS version, app version, language, and the region your internet connection comes from). We send it no name, no answers and nothing you entered. It is not used to follow you across other apps or websites.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Health information

We do not collect, share or sell health information. What you record about your health in Evenphase never leaves your iPhone unless you choose to export or share it yourself. Because we never receive it, we cannot access it, disclose it or restore it for you.

Why we are allowed to use this information

We use purchase information to provide the subscription you bought (performance of a contract) and anonymous information about the subscription screen to understand how the app is doing (our legitimate interest). Superwall is based in the United States and processes this information on our behalf, under its data processing terms.

How long it is kept

What you enter stays on your iPhone until you delete it or delete the app. Purchase records are kept by Apple and Superwall for as long as the law and the operation of the subscription require.

Your choices and rights

  • Export: in the app, open Settings, then "Export or delete my data". It works even without a subscription.
  • Delete: in the same place, choose "Delete all my data". Deleting the app also removes it.
  • Subscription: manage or cancel it in iPhone Settings, under your Apple ID, then Subscriptions.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for access to, correction or deletion of the purchase information linked to your random identifier, and to object to its use. Write to us and we will help. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos).

Adults only

Evenphase is for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children.

Not medical advice, not an emergency service

Evenphase is a diary. It helps you record and see your own days. It does not diagnose any condition, score your symptoms or recommend treatment. Nobody monitors what you write in the app, and the app cannot contact anyone for you. If you are in danger or thinking about ending your life, call your local emergency number or a crisis line (in the United States, call or text 988).

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
