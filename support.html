Common questions

I paid, but the app still shows the subscription screen

On the subscription screen, tap "Restore". Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel?

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Evenphase. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the Evenphase purchase.

My reminder doesn't arrive

Open iPhone Settings, then Notifications, then Evenphase, and turn on "Allow Notifications". Then open the app once.

I don't have periods. Can I still use it?

Yes. On Today, tap "Mark a cycle start" on the day a new cycle seems to begin for you. Your cycle view counts from there.

I missed a day

Open the Cycle tab and tap the empty day to fill it in. Days filled in later are marked in your report.

Where is my data? Can I move it to a new iPhone?

Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. It moves to a new iPhone when you restore from your iPhone backup. You can also make a copy any time: in the app, open Settings, then "Export or delete my data".

How do I delete everything?

In the app, open Settings, then "Export or delete my data", then "Delete all my data". This cannot be undone.

Not medical advice

Evenphase is a diary. It does not diagnose any condition and does not give medical advice. For questions about your health or a treatment, speak to a clinician.
