Evenphase

Finally make sense of your cycle

Evenphase is an iPhone app for people whose mood and body change with their cycle, including PMDD, PMS and PME. A private daily diary, a clear record for your appointments, and a plan for the hard days. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.
